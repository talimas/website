Some sentences do not ask to be understood immediately. They ask for a pocket, a walk, and a second encounter in another season.

---

## A modest method

Copy it. Date it. Write beside it what it changed in the room. Leave enough space for it to change again.

Then make tea and go back to [[Garden/Mornings|the morning]].
